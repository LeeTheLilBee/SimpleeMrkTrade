# The Grounds GRD163–171 — My Home, The Daily Grounds and Property Health

This source-only expansion builds the requested role-specific experience **from existing authoritative Grounds physical records**. The additional endpoints are served through the normal per-request certified-Tower-ready WSGI boundary, with exact property/unit/lease checks on each domain read. No new paid service, private host, external provider, public route, bank source, Vault file access, real tenant or active release permission.

- `GET /grounds/api/my-home?property_ref=&unit_ref=` (resident only): independently checks current lease membership and returns property/unit label, lease dates, resident's own maintenance counts, in-app unread notice count and ONLY their own linked appointment metadata. Current missing Teller invoice remains an UNKNOWN amount; Vault retrieval, legal notice and payment are false. No other household member's private work or appointment is sent.
- `GET /grounds/api/daily?property_ref=` (owner/property manager/maintenance supervisor): source-only priority queue: unreviewed urgent work, open work older than a **descriptive** three days (not a legal SLA), pending appointment decisions, due preventive tasks, open inspections, and pending *local* notification intents. Maintenance supervisor gets NULL (not zero) for owner/manager-only turnover counts. Urgent metadata excludes descriptions/resident identities; no provider dispatch claim.
- `GET /grounds/api/property-health?property_ref=` (owner/property manager/regional manager): unit lifecycle occupancy, open work, urgent/turnover/serious finding counts, new work in the most recent 30 vs previous 30 days, count of repeated unit/category request groups, and an explicitly bounded max-200 first-received-duration sample (not a performance guarantee or diagnosis). No rent amount, collections, available capital or resident identifier.
- `GET /grounds/api/owner-portfolio` (owner only, no caller-supplied scope): enumerates at most 50 currently Tower-granted property health summaries, reports total authorized scope and whether truncated, refuses >500 property grants until a reviewed pagination contract exists. No external Clouds publication, OB access or money calculation.

Browser additions use progressively disclosed dark-glass visual tiles, no HTML injection, exact per-context fetch generation, a nonpersistent larger-text setting and clear caveats for absent Teller/Vault/provider certification. Disabled roles do not receive endpoints or panel data. Existing rent, leasing, safety, physical, resident work and independent operational-release controls remain unchanged.

Tests: `test_grounds_experience.py` covers active/ended lease, unrelated/current same-unit resident, staff and property denial; owner/regional vs manager/supervisor grants; source-only money and provider flags; repeat work grouping. `test_grounds_web.py` checks actual protected HTTP endpoints including unexpected query/unknown actor denial. `test_grounds_postgres.py` exercises the same projections through actual WSGI and disposable PostgreSQL. Exact source and PostgreSQL CI required before merging to parent #51. Live owner acceptance, certified Tower runtime and independent operational release, protected private DB/restore, actual Teller/Vault, provider delivery and legal/accessibility review remain separate unmet gates.
