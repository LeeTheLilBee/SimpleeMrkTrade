# OBUX061–065 — Interchangeable Themes + Beta Surface Cleanup

The OBUX056–060 Dashboard layout is preserved.

## Dashboard structure retained

- Soulaana Right Now
- Account Snapshot
- Since You Were Here
- Your Activity
- Market Now
- Operating Loop
- My OB

## Themes

Three appearance-only themes are available:

1. Obsidian Plum
2. Velvet Night
3. Eclipse Gold

The theme preference is remembered in browser storage.

Theme choice does not alter market truth, rankings, modes, permissions,
contract choice, broker behavior, session truth, or Soulaana decision logic.

## Normal beta surface cleanup

The product surface rejects legacy proof/verification chrome:

- Room-Level Data Polish
- mission-account bars
- engine-feed status bars
- data-status bars
- snapshot verification panels

Owner Dashboard and Owner Console are excluded from the normal beta
surface cleanup.

## Progressive disclosure

The left hover room navigation remains.

Redundant permanent top bars are hidden on normal beta surfaces.

A single compact control opens a right-side drawer containing:

- current room
- current mode
- Tower Protected
- Live Auto Locked
- Appearance
- Dashboard / My OB
- beta feedback
- Beta Guide
- What Changed
- Back to Tower
- Sign out of OB
