# BuyBox Offer Lab — BBX092–096\n\n## Scope\n\nOffer Lab is a private owner analysis room for modeling possible acquisition terms against the exact currently saved BuyBox opportunity revision. It is universal across the seven BuyBox verticals and is intentionally non-executing.\n\nA recorded scenario may include proposed purchase price, earnest money, requested seller credit, diligence period, financing contingency, planned closing date, and optional links to current source-backed financing and insurance records.\n\n## Authority boundary\n\nAn Offer Lab scenario is not an offer, LOI, purchase contract, seller communication, Tower approval, Teller readiness determination, lender instruction, insurer instruction, or authorization to move money. Recording or correcting a scenario cannot advance lifecycle state.\n\nEvery record keeps transmitted_to_seller=false, is_loi=false, is_contract=false, authorizes_offer=false, authorizes_purchase=false, and authorizes_money=false. Tower authority remains absent and Teller readiness remains UNKNOWN until their owning systems separately provide authenticated authority.\n\n## Source binding and staleness\n\nCreation requires the exact persisted BuyBox opportunity revision and input snapshot digest. Asking-price changes, later material-source changes, superseded financing records, changed financing source hashes, expired financing terms, superseded insurance records, changed insurance source hashes, unknown coverage dates, and quote/coverage dates that do not span the proposed close all create explicit recheck flags.\n\nCorrections are append-only: a new private scenario supersedes the earlier record without rewriting history.\n\n## Soulaana\n\nSoulaana may explain scenario math, recheck flags, cited financing/insurance context, and why a scenario is stale. She may not recommend a winning scenario, contact a seller, produce a binding instrument, grant Tower authority, assert Teller readiness, or authorize acquisition.\n\n## Release status\n\nSource-only. No live Tower sender/receiver activation, Teller readiness response, seller communication, LOI generation, e-signature, lender/insurer request, payment, broker action, public BuyBox deployment, or paid Render resource is created by BBX092–096. The owner source-only hold remains binding.\n
