# BuyBox Intelligence Expansion — BBX107–130

## Purpose

BBX107–130 expands the completed BuyBox acquisition workspace into a learning, portfolio, strategy and institutional-memory system without weakening its source/authority boundaries.

The expansion is intentionally built as one **Intelligence Studio** instead of disconnected mini-products. Every projection is derived from persisted BuyBox records, owner-authored research, existing evidence, financing, negotiations, tasks, lifecycle history and trusted external-proof state.

## Expansion map

1. **Acquisition Command Map** — clusters current saved opportunities by recorded city/region and vertical. No geocoding or invented coordinates.
2. **Portfolio Fit Engine** — compares a deal to the owner thesis and current saved pipeline. Analytical only.
3. **Acquisition Timeline Simulator** — combines tasks, financing expirations and owner-recorded dated intelligence items.
4. **Negotiation Intelligence** — summarizes recorded negotiation events and amounts; never infers seller motive.
5. **Deal DNA** — descriptive pattern matching against the owner's own saved acquisition history.
6. **Predicted vs Actual Loop** — compares BuyBox's source-based normalized economics with separately owner-recorded post-close actuals.
7. **Seller / Broker History** — cross-deal counterparty history, roles and record counts. No invented reliability rating.
8. **Lender Library** — historical financing sources/programs from actual recorded financing options. No current availability claim.
9. **Inspection & Repair Intelligence** — owner-recorded CapEx/defect ledger with severity, cost estimate, date and source note.
10. **Acquisition Calendar** — portfolio-wide dated acquisition obligations.
11. **Watchtower** — surfaces existing Saved Search / Opportunity Radar strategies. It remains owner-run until an approved external listing feed exists.
12. **Opportunity Inbox** — creates a provisional DISCOVERED opportunity from an owner-entered raw note and optional HTTP(S) source; no extracted fact is silently verified.
13. **Team Deal Lanes** — owner-recorded role/work/status lanes. They are workflow assignments, not access grants.
14. **Owner / IC Decision Packet** — dedicated human-review packet with analysis, findings, portfolio fit, timeline, Why Not questions and integration state. Never an approval instrument.
15. **Vertical Acquisition Playbooks** — deterministic per-vertical diligence/transition prompts. They do not claim legal requirements.
16. **Market Intelligence Layer** — source-attributed owner market observations. No external market feed is implied.
17. **Geographic Expansion Intelligence** — shows regions where multiple vertical acquisition records are accumulating.
18. **Capital Deployment Board** — displays deal requirements and whether authenticated Teller proof exists. No direct OB access or balance display.
19. **What-If Portfolio Builder** — combines 1–8 selected opportunities for asking-price, vertical and regional concentration analysis. Capital readiness is never assumed.
20. **Acquisition Autopsy** — preserves owner-recorded outcome/reason history for rejected, lost, withdrawn or acquired opportunities.
21. **Why Not? Mode** — challenge questions generated only from current rule findings and recorded CapEx items.
22. **Owner Thesis Board** — persisted owner priorities, preferred regions, avoid list, sequence and notes. It informs strategy views but does not override evidence/rules.
23. **Community Impact Lens** — separately records impact dimensions such as local jobs, housing quality, food access or green space. It explicitly does not alter financial scoring.
24. **Deal Story View** — chronological merge of BuyBox event history and Intelligence Studio records.
25. **Mobile Capture Mode** — fast owner field-note capture. Existing protected document intake remains the path for photos/files.
26. **Acquisition Knowledge Graph** — derives opportunity/counterparty/lender relationships from persisted records only.

## Security and authority doctrine

- Intelligence records are owner research/workflow records, not evidence.
- Intelligence records never promote CLAIMED/RECEIVED evidence to documentary support.
- The Studio cannot change Teller, Tower, Vault, Grounds or ATM authority.
- No external feed, geocoder, lender availability, demographic statistic, zoning fact, crime statistic, property tax, permit, seller reliability rating or current market fact is fabricated.
- Community impact remains transparent and separate from financial underwriting.
- Team lanes are not user permissions.
- Capital Board never reads OB directly and never displays a deployable balance without authenticated Teller proof.
- Owner/IC packets never sign, transmit, fund, close or authorize an acquisition.

## External extensions deliberately left connector-gated

Future approved connectors may enrich the Market Intelligence Layer (public/property/permit/zoning/demographic sources), Watchtower (listing feeds), Command Map (geocoding/map tiles), mobile capture (camera/file upload convenience), and Predicted-vs-Actual (permissioned Grounds/SimpleeOnTheGo operating snapshots). Those integrations must retain source timestamps, provenance, privacy controls and Tower/Teller/Grounds boundaries.

## Release status

Source expansion only. No paid Render resources, production secrets, external listing subscriptions, map provider, market-data subscription, user invitations, live Teller/Tower/Vault/Grounds/ATM authority, or public BuyBox deployment is created by BBX107–130.
