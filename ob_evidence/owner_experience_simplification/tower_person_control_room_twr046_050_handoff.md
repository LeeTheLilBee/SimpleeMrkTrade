## Product rule

The person room becomes the owner-facing control surface behind People + seats.

## TWR046 — Person Room foundation

Adds a deeper protected person-control surface behind each person/seat.

## TWR047 — Designation + responsibilities

Adds draft-only controls for:

- designation
- organizational lane
- responsibilities
- status planning

## TWR048 — Access

Adds Tower and Observatory access-review surfaces.

No access is granted or revoked.

## TWR049 — Activity + history

Introduces the visible shape of the future append-only Tower person activity history.

## TWR050 — Owner actions

Adds explicit owner actions for:

- change designation
- change access
- change responsibilities
- freeze/suspension draft
- restore draft
- paperwork review
- history review

## Safety doctrine

Owner action → Tower validation → draft change request → owner review → future authorized apply layer.

TWR046–TWR050 does not create accounts, send invites, grant permissions, revoke permissions, suspend real users, unlock Live Auto, execute broker actions, or take capital action.
