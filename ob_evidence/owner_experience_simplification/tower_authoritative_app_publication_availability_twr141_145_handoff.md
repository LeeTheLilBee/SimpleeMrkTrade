# THE TOWER — TWR141–TWR145

## Authoritative App Publication + Availability

TWR141–TWR145 separates app registration from actual operating reality.

The canonical Tower registry remains registration and route metadata only.

A separate integrity-verified publication authority can prove:

- implementation
- publication
- environment availability
- health verification

Current hosted owner identity remains the entitlement authority.

Actual per-request authorization remains separate.

## Launchability

An app is product-level launchable only when all of the following are verified:

1. registered
2. implemented
3. published
4. fresh environment availability
5. fresh health verification
6. current owner entitlement
7. real Tower launch route

Launchability does not bypass:

- owner session verification
- request authorization
- step-up

## Locked boundaries

Registration does not imply implementation.

Implementation does not imply publication.

Publication does not imply availability.

Availability does not imply health.

Health does not imply entitlement.

Entitlement does not imply authorization.

Future registry entries do not become product cards or launchable apps.

Stale availability and health receipts fail closed.

Live Auto, broker execution, capital movement, release execution, and Manual Live authorization remain locked.
