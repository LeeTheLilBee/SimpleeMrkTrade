## What this prepares

GP013 prepares the future owner authorization packet for the controlled owner
walkthrough after GP012 created the closed controlled-run gate.

- Decision options
- Required fields

- Authorization requirements

## Important boundary

This package prepares the packet only. It does not grant owner authorization,
sign the packet, record an authorization decision, open the controlled-run gate,
authorize the controlled run, start the walkthrough, accept the walkthrough,
open live routes as evidence, repair Tower return/session continuity, redeploy
Render, authorize production deployment, claim staging readiness, submit to
broker, move real capital, enable direct execution, enable automated execution,
mutate permissions, reveal secrets, or unlock Live Auto.
