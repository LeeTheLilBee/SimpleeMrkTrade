THE PROCESS

From discovery to trusted observation

  1. 01DiscoverFind a provider, official directory or research source.
  2. 02Review rightsCheck product, venue, business use, display, redistribution and expiry.
  3. 03ConfigureServer-held credentials and independent source/product mapping.
  4. 04VerifyAuthenticated feed, timestamp, coverage, quotas and canonical market clock.
  5. 05Owner approvalFresh Tower step-up and an auditable, narrow read-only decision.
  6. 06ObserveCheck traffic, quote freshness, options identity, and source conflicts.
  7. 07Hold or revokeIsolate failing feeds; never silently replace live data with delayed prices.
